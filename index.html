About Me

I am a UK-based Supply Chain and Logistics professional who leads teams in operational environments. I relocated from India to the UK in 2023 to complete an MBA in Supply Chain Management, and have since rebuilt my people-leadership experience inside UK fulfilment and retail operations.

My background combines practical experience at Amazon UK, Sainsbury’s, CN Foods, and Shekhawati Sales & Transportation with an MBA in Supply Chain Management from the University of East London. I have worked with WMS, RF scanning, stock control, order fulfilment, dispatch coordination, KPI tracking, quality control, 5S routines, and process improvement. I am confident working in fast-paced operational environments where accuracy, productivity, safety, and customer service are essential.

Before moving to the UK I directly managed a small sales and service team for three years at a transportation business, owning client escalations, service delivery and delay resolution while growing revenue by 30% and retaining 95% of 50+ accounts. I am now focused on Team Leader, Shift Supervisor, Inventory Supervisor and Assistant Operations Manager roles, where operational credibility on the floor and accountability for a team’s performance matter equally.
