About

MBA Supply Chain Management

I work in Inbound Operations at Amazon EMA5 in Kettering, where I perform Process Guide duties as part of my permanent Warehouse Operative role and help coordinate 20–40 associates per shift.

I allocate work based on priorities and associate training, organise staggered breaks and coordinate labour share. I monitor productivity and quality, provide refresher guidance, escalate operational issues and give shift leadership hourly updates and end-of-shift handovers.

Improvements implemented from 13 October 2026 include maintaining 50% staffing coverage during breaks through a fair rotating rota, contributing to a 30% reduction in errors through refresher guidance, and raising handover readiness from 30% to at least 95%. I prepare processable volume and empty totes for 50–60 minutes of work, with bins cleaned and 5S and housekeeping completed, to support the next shift’s fast start.

At Amazon EDI4, I achieved 100% picking accuracy across 3,959 units in Outbound. At CN Foods, I coordinated 10+ operatives during peak-period shift lead cover. At Shekhawati Sales & Transportation, I managed a four-person sales and service team.

I hold an MBA in Supply Chain Management from the University of East London. My experience also includes inventory control, stock reconciliation and Excel reporting. I’m targeting Team Leader, Shift Supervisor and Warehouse Supervisor roles, and I’m open to relevant logistics and inventory opportunities.
