Keeping the inbound line running through breaks

I introduced staggered break coverage while performing Process Guide duties at Amazon EMA5 Inbound.

What needed to change

When stations took breaks at the same time, the inbound line could stop. Coverage mattered for both the stations and the people who kept them supplied.

What I did

I gave neighbouring stations alternating break slots and split support staff evenly between the two groups. I rotated the slots so the same people did not always take the earlier or later break.

What happened

Around half the team stayed on the floor during each break slot, allowing the inbound line to keep running.
